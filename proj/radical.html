Founding Intern — Radical

Visit Radical on TikTok →

Since May 2026, I've been a Founding Intern at Radical, a Bay Area fashion brand building its presence on social media. As one of the first people to help shape the brand's content strategy, I've been in it from the start: pitching ideas, writing scripts, and helping define what Radical looks and sounds like online.

My day-to-day work involves developing content ideas and scripting social media videos, with a focus on short-form video that connects with a style-conscious Bay Area audience. I also storyboard campaign assets before we shoot so each piece of content has a clear concept and direction going in.

A big part of my role has been leading outreach for Radical's 60-day video challenge initiative, a campaign aimed at growing the brand's community and driving sign-ups across the Bay Area. This has meant coordinating with creators, managing timelines, and keeping momentum going across multiple moving pieces.

Skills: Content Strategy · Short-Form Video · Scriptwriting · Campaign Outreach · Brand Development
