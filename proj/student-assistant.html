Student Assistant

University of California, Berkeley

Since May 2026, I've been working as a Student Assistant at UC Berkeley, supporting front desk and facilities operations for a campus building. This role keeps me grounded in the day-to-day rhythms of university life while building skills in communication, logistics, and team coordination.

My responsibilities include directing guests and conference attendees, managing incoming calls, and assisting with lockouts. All of it requires staying calm, organized, and solutions-focused in a busy and unpredictable environment. I also coordinate with internal teams on room stocking and maintenance to make sure spaces are ready and operational.

Working at the front desk of a university building means being the first point of contact for a wide range of people, from students and staff to outside visitors and event coordinators. It's sharpened my ability to communicate clearly, prioritize on the fly, and represent the university professionally in every interaction.

Skills: Front Office Operations · Communication · Multitasking · Stakeholder Coordination
