GBO Leader — Golden Bear Orientation

University of California, Berkeley

From April to August 2025, I served as a Golden Bear Orientation (GBO) Leader at UC Berkeley. It's one of the student-facing roles that shapes how thousands of new Bears experience their very first days on campus, and honestly one of the most rewarding things I've done at Berkeley.

As a GBO Leader, I facilitated small group orientation sessions for incoming students: leading discussions, answering questions, and helping new students find their footing in a big, sometimes overwhelming university. A big part of the job was creating an environment where students felt comfortable being themselves and connecting with each other early on.

I also organized and led community-building activities designed to help students bond across different backgrounds and majors. Having gone through the transfer experience myself, I brought a specific perspective to the role, especially when it came to helping transfer students who often navigate orientation with a different set of concerns than traditional freshmen.

Skills: Facilitation · Community Building · Public Speaking · Student Support · Event Coordination
