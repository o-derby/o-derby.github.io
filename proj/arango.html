Arango AI

Technical Product Marketing Intern · San Francisco, CA · Aug 2026 – Present

Arango AI — formerly ArangoDB — is a San Francisco company building developer infrastructure. I joined as a Technical Product Marketing Intern, which puts me in the gap between the people building the product and the developers who need to be convinced it's worth their time.

A lot of my work has been growing the company's Reddit presence from an account with nothing on it. Reddit is an unusually unforgiving channel for a technical product: the audience can smell marketing instantly, and a post that reads like a press release gets buried or mocked. Building from zero means the only thing that works is actually being useful in threads where the problem you solve is already being discussed.

That led to building an internal tool to track mentions of Arango across Reddit threads, so we can find those conversations as they happen rather than stumbling on them a week late. It's the sort of thing that's obvious once you've spent a few weeks manually searching for the same terms and realising a script should be doing it.

I also spend real time testing the actual Arango software. Marketing a developer tool you haven't used produces exactly the kind of content developers ignore — you end up describing features instead of problems. Using it is how you find out which parts are genuinely good and which parts a skeptical engineer would poke at first.

Skills: Technical Product Marketing · Developer Marketing · Community Growth · Internal Tooling
