Growth & GTM Intern — Osmo

Visit Osmo →

From January to May 2026, I worked as a Growth & GTM Intern at Osmo, an AI motion graphics startup based in San Francisco. Osmo builds tools that let creators generate high-quality motion graphics and video content using AI. It's a fast-moving space that requires sharp instincts around both product and audience.

My work spanned go-to-market strategy, market research, and client-facing advertising support. I analyzed user needs and proposed data-driven marketing campaigns and feature enhancements to help align the product roadmap with what real users were asking for. One of the highlights: I helped develop an ad that reached over 200,000 views within its first 24 hours.

On the research side, I built and maintained a database of 60+ potential social media promoters to support upcoming product launches and improve the team's influencer outreach pipeline. This involved vetting creators by niche, engagement rate, and audience fit so the team had a ready-to-go list for future campaigns.

This role gave me hands-on experience applying marketing strategy in a fast-paced startup environment, from ideation to execution, and sharpened my ability to connect product features to real market opportunities.

Skills: Go-to-Market Strategy · Market Research · Content Strategy · Growth Marketing · Data Analysis
