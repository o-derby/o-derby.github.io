E-Commerce Clerk

Ralphs (Kroger Co.) · Anaheim Hills, CA

From July 2022 to July 2024, I worked as an E-Commerce Clerk at Ralphs in Anaheim Hills, a high-volume grocery store where I spent two years across multiple departments including pick-up, cashier, and floral. It was my first long-term job and it taught me more about real-world work than I expected.

In e-commerce specifically, I managed online order fulfillment from start to finish: picking orders, substituting when items were out of stock, and coordinating pickup handoffs. During peak hours, I learned to prioritize and adapt on the fly to keep turnaround times low and customers satisfied, even when the floor was slammed.

Working across departments gave me a broad view of how a large retail operation runs, from inventory and supply to customer-facing service. The floral department in particular required creativity and attention to detail, while cashiering sharpened my speed, accuracy, and ability to handle customer issues quickly and professionally.

Two years in a fast-paced, team-dependent environment built habits that have carried into everything I've done since: staying calm under pressure, communicating clearly with teammates, and taking ownership of the work in front of me regardless of the task.

Skills: Customer Service · E-Commerce Operations · Time Management · Adaptability · Team Collaboration
