Data Science Transfer Council

UC Berkeley College of Computing, Data Science, and Society

Since March 2026, I've been a member of the Data Science Transfer Council (DSTC) at UC Berkeley's College of Computing, Data Science, and Society (CDSS). The council was created to give transfer students a direct voice in shaping the programs and resources built for incoming transfers in the data science space.

My work on the council involves advising on the structure and content of new student programming, covering everything from orientation materials to community-building initiatives, with a focus on making sure incoming transfer students feel supported and prepared from day one. As a transfer student myself, I bring a firsthand perspective on the gaps and needs that often go unaddressed in traditional onboarding.

This role sits at the intersection of community advocacy and program development, and it's given me experience collaborating with faculty, staff, and student leaders to influence how a major college at Berkeley serves its transfer population.

Skills: Community Outreach · Program Development · Student Advocacy · Cross-functional Collaboration
