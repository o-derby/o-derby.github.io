Co-Founder — Kinetik

Berkeley, CA · Jan 2026 – Present

Kinetik is an early-stage startup I co-founded in January 2026. We're using computer vision to track patient movement and progress in remote physical therapy — the part of recovery that currently happens unsupervised, where a patient is at home doing exercises with no way for anyone to know whether the form is right or whether they're actually improving.

As a co-founder at this stage, the work doesn't split cleanly into a single function. I contribute across product, model development, and clinical and regulatory research. That last piece has been the most unexpected education — building anything that touches patient care means the technical problem and the compliance problem are the same problem, and you can't defer one until the other is solved.

It's been the most complete thing I've worked on: computer vision and machine learning on one side, real clinical constraints on the other, and the constant early-stage question of whether the thing you're building is the thing anyone actually needs.

Skills: Computer Vision · Machine Learning · Product Development · Clinical & Regulatory Research
