UC Berkeley Transfer Student Center

Visit Offical UC Berkeley TSC Instagram Here!

As a Peer Advocate Lead for the official UC Berkeley Transfer Student Center, I work on the Communications Team. With this, my responsibilities include managing the center's official social media pages. I get to also develop bi-weekly newsletters for 7000+ tranfer students utilizing a program called MailChimp.

Content-wise, I get to develop social media campaigns. One of my favorites is an animated trivia series, which I call the "Did you know?" series where I voice, edit (using Adobe After Effects), and draw (using Art Studio Pro) engaing animations of campus history/traditions. Here is an example.

Additionally, I also interview students around campus to help create a sense of community among transfer students. This has helped me learn effective communication and presentation skills in a film setting. Here is an example.

One of the bigger moments this past year was The Big Give, UC Berkeley's annual fundraising campaign. I helped coordinate our communications push around the event, working on outreach content and messaging to drive donations to the Transfer Student Center. Our efforts helped raise over $12,000 in a single campaign, which goes directly toward supporting transfer students at Berkeley. Here is one of the videos from the campaign.
