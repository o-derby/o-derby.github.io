Fitness Rehab App Startup

Spring 2026 • UC Berkeley Startup Course

Overview

Building a mobile fitness app that provides customized workout plans for users with injuries, disabilities, or specific fitness goals. Leading go-to-market strategy and user research as part of a team with engineering students.

My Role

Go-to-Market Strategy: Defining customer segments, positioning, and launch strategy for the app

User Research: Conducting interviews with athletes, physical therapists, and people recovering from injuries to understand pain points and validate product-market fit

Competitive Analysis: Analyzing competitor apps to identify market gaps and differentiation opportunities

Product Strategy: Working with engineering team to prioritize features based on user needs and market insights

Key Insights (So Far)

Through user interviews, we've learned that:
• People recovering from injuries struggle to find safe, personalized workout routines
• Most fitness apps don't account for physical limitations or injury recovery timelines
• Users want confidence that they won't re-injure themselves when returning to exercise
• Physical therapists often lack tools to give patients structured workout plans

Tools & Methods

User interviews, competitive analysis, market research, customer segmentation, product positioning

Status

In Progress - Conducting user research and building product strategy (Spring 2026)
